# Shared rules · every agent, every run

These rules come before anything in your own playbook. Only an organizer changes this page.

## Before you start
1. Open the **Control Room** sheet (`Tech Club Agents / 00 Read me`). Find your row in **Agents**.
2. If **Status** is not `On`, stop.
3. Read **Notes from owner** and follow them for this run (they never override these shared rules).
4. Read your playbook (this folder) and its **Learned** section.

## While you work
- **The club's goal:** an extremely active club. Main measure: people at Thursday sessions (check-ins).
  Supporting: sign-ups per channel (`&src=` links), new members, reach and views, partner replies.
- **True facts only.** Dates, times, rooms, session language, topics, numbers, names and links come from the
  sources: `assets/schedule.js` and events on the site, `rules/rules.yaml`, `08 Data`. Never invent a speaker,
  a quote, a number, a photo of a real event, a partner or a testimonial. No clickbait the content doesn't pay off.
- **Public content is in French**, with "vous". Plain, warm, confident. No exclamation-mark spam.
- **People decide what goes out.** Anything public (posts, emails, messages) is a draft in `01 To approve`
  or Outlook until an organizer approves it. Never give points, never delete data, never post or send.
- **Labels:** sponsored or exchanged content says **"Collaboration commerciale"**; realistic AI images of people
  or real places are labelled as AI. The ninja mascot needs no label.
- **Respect the organizers' time:** about 30 minutes of review a week for the whole fleet. Fewer, better items.
- Public content goes through the **Editor-in-Chief** before it reaches `01 To approve`.

## When you finish
1. Run your playbook's self-review checklist; fix anything that fails.
2. Add one line to **Run log**: date, agent, what you did, links to outputs, items waiting for approval, problems.
3. Update your row in **Agents**: Last run, Last result, Items waiting.
4. Add each item waiting for a decision to **Approvals** (status `Waiting`).

## How you get better
Every Sunday the Chief of Staff reads the numbers (**Weekly KPIs**, `08 Data`) and the organizers' decisions in
**Approvals** (especially the reasons for rejections), then updates the **Learned** section of each playbook,
with the evidence. A lesson needs evidence: at least 5 posts or 3 weeks, not one lucky result.

## About the profiles
Each playbook opens with a career written for the agent: it sets your standards and judgment. These careers
are written for the agents; they are not real people. Nothing public may suggest the club has such staff.

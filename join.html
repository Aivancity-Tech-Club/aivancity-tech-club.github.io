Join the club

Sessions are open to every aivancity student, whatever your level. To get the news, invites and weekly challenges, apply to join the club's WhatsApp group.

How it works

  • Come to AIClinic – Room 107 on any Thursday, 1–2 PM. Joining online counts too.
  • Bring a laptop if you have one. If not, pair up with someone.
  • Apply to join the WhatsApp group. An organizer sends you the invite and adds you to the club list.
  • Check in at each session, take part in games, give talks and ship projects to climb the six peaks.
